# EFFECTIVE SCHOOL DAY RULES

Decoupled criteria for active school days in Attendance V2.

## Definition
An **Effective Day** is any date where:
- Standard class activities are scheduled.
- Attendance records are mandatory and expected for murid.
- Writing/saving records is authorized (unless the period is locked).

## Rules Checklist
1. **Default School Days**: Monday through Friday are effective. Saturday is effective only in `6days` format.
2. **Weekend Rules**: Sunday is always non-effective. Saturday in `5days` format is non-effective.
3. **Holiday Records**: Explicitly registered holidays (national or custom) are non-effective.
4. **Events Override**: Calendar events scheduled for the school or specific classes make the day effective (even if it falls on a weekend or holiday).
5. **Administrative Closures**: Period locks or forced closures override all other rules, making days non-effective or read-only.
